Day 1 — Kubernetes Foundation

Learned Kubernetes architecture including nodes, pods, deployments, services, namespaces, labels and selectors.

Day 3 — Pods and Deployments

Created pods and deployments while learning scaling, rolling updates and self-healing.

Day 4 — Networking and Services
