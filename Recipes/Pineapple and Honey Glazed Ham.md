# 🍍 Pineapple & Honey Glazed Ham (Holiday Recipe)
![[Pineapple and Honey Ham.png]]

## 📝 Ingredients

- **10–12 lb fully cooked ham**
    **20 oz canned pineapple slices** (with juice)
     **¼ cup honey**
    - **½ cup brown sugar**
    - **½ tsp ground cloves**
    - **½ tsp cinnamon**
    - **1 tbsp cornstarch**
   - **1 tbsp water**
- **Maraschino cherries** (for topping)

---

## 🍳 Instructions

### **1. Preheat the oven**
- Set oven to **325°F (163°C)**

---
### **2. Make the glaze**
- In a saucepan, combine:
        - Pineapple juice
        - Brown sugar
        - Ground cloves
        - Cinnamon
   - Stir well until blended
- Add **honey** and continue stirring

---

### **3. Thicken the glaze**
- In a small bowl, mix:
       - **1 tbsp cornstarch**
       - **1 tbsp cold water**
       - Make a smooth slurry
    - Pour slurry **slowly** into the glaze while stirring
    - Cook over **medium–low heat**, stirring constantly
    - When thickened to your liking, remove from heat
    
---

### **4. Prepare the ham**

- Use a **10–12 lb fully cooked ham**
   - If spiral-cut, insert pineapple slices between the layers
    Secure with toothpicks
- Pour glaze generously over the ham

---

### **5. Bake the ham**

- Place ham in a roasting pan
- Cover with foil, leaving **space above the ham**
   - Bake at **325°F** for **1.5 hours**
- **Baste every 30 minutes** with glaze

---

### **6. Finish glazing**

- After 1.5 hours:
    - Remove foil
    - Add more glaze
    - Return to oven **uncovered**
   - Bake **another 1 hour** or until fully heated and glossy
- Glaze occasionally for best shine

---
### **7. Rest & serve**
- Remove from oven
    Let rest a few minutes
   - Garnish with **maraschino cherries**
- Slice and serve warm

---

## 🍽️ Tips
- Foil tent prevents burning while keeping ham moist
    - Cornstarch slurry ensures a thick, sticky glaze
    - Pineapple slices help baste the ham as it cooks
    - For deeper color, broil 2–3 minutes at the very end (watch closely)
  

---
## 🔗 Notes

- Perfect centerpiece for Thanksgiving, Christmas, Easter
    - Works with spiral cut or whole ham
    - Extra glaze can be served as a sauce
