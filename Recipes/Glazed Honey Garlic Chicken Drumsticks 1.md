# 🛒 Ingredients

### Chicken

- 2.2 lb (1 kg) chicken drumsticks
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- Salt, to taste
- Black pepper, to taste
### Honey Garlic Sauce
- ¾ cup (200 ml) honey
- 1 oz (30 g) butter
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- ⅔ cup (150 ml) ketchup
- ¼ cup (60 ml) soy sauce
### Garnish

- 1 tbsp sesame seeds
- Fresh parsley, chopped

---
# 🍳 Equipment
- Large oven-safe skillet or frying pan
- Mixing spoon
- Cutting board
- Knife
- Oven

---
# 👨‍🍳 Instructions
## Step 1 — Brown the Chicken

1. Heat the vegetable oil in a large skillet over **medium heat**.
2. Season the chicken drumsticks with **salt and black pepper**.
3. Cook for **2–3 minutes per side**, just until lightly browned.
4. Remove the drumsticks and set aside.

---
## Step 2 — Make the Sauce

Using the same pan:
1. Reduce the heat to **low**.
2. Add:
   - Honey
   - Butter
   - Minced garlic
   - Ketchup
   - Soy sauce
3. Stir continuously until the butter melts and the sauce becomes smooth.

---
## Step 3 — Coat the Chicken
1. Return the browned drumsticks to the pan.
2. Spoon the sauce over each drumstick until fully coated.

---
## Step 4 — Bake
1. Preheat oven to **350°F (180°C)**.
2. Transfer the pan to the oven.
3. Bake for **18–20 minutes**, or until the chicken reaches an internal temperature of **165°F (74°C)**.
4. Baste the drumsticks once during baking for extra flavor.

---
## Step 5 — Garnish & Serve
Sprinkle with:

- Sesame seeds
- Fresh chopped parsley

Serve immediately while hot.

# 💡 Chef's Tips

- Marinate the chicken in the sauce for **2–4 hours** before cooking for even more flavor.
- For crispier skin, broil the drumsticks for **2–3 minutes** after baking.
- Add a pinch of red pepper flakes for a sweet-and-spicy version.
- Fresh garlic provides the best flavor.

---
# 🧊 Storage
**Refrigerator**
- Store in an airtight container for up to **4 days**.
**Freezer**
- Freeze for up to **3 months**.

- Oven: 350°F (175°C) for 10–15 minutes
- Microwave: 2–3 minutes

# ⭐ Recipe Notes

This recipe combines sweet honey, savory soy sauce, garlic, and ketchup into a rich glaze that caramelizes beautifully in the oven. Browning the drumsticks before baking helps develop flavor and creates a golden exterior while keeping the meat juicy and tender.
