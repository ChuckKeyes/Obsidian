# 🍠 Candied Yams (Stovetop)

**Video Tutorial:**  
🔗 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fOhMYgfOtmM

---

## 📝 Ingredients
- 2 lb sweet potatoes (peeled & chopped)
- ¾ cup brown sugar
- 1 cup white sugar
- 2 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp nutmeg
- ¼ tsp ginger
- ¼ tsp salt
- 2 tbsp vanilla extract
- 1 stick butter
- Juice of 1 orange

---

## 🍳 Instructions

### **1. Prep the Sweet Potatoes**
(From video 2:00 – 3:29)
- Peel sweet potatoes  
- Cut into thick rounds or chunks  

---

### **2. Make the Candied Syrup**
(From video 3:29 – 5:55)
- Melt butter in a pot  
- Add brown sugar, white sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger, salt  
- Add orange juice  
- Stir until smooth and melted  

---

### **3. Add Sweet Potatoes & Simmer**
(From video 5:55 – 9:59)
- Add yams to the pot  
- Coat with syrup  
- Cover and simmer on low 35–45 minutes  
- Flip gently every 10 minutes  
- Cook until tender but not mushy  

---

### **4. Add Vanilla**
- Turn off heat  
- Stir in vanilla extract  
- Spoon syrup over the yams  

---

### **5. Serve**
(From video 9:59 – END)
- Move yams to a dish  
- Pour syrup over top  
- Let them set to thicken  

---

## 🍽️ Tips
- Do NOT add water  
- Don’t stir aggressively—flip gently  
- Add vanilla at the end (prevents burning)  
- If syrup is too thick, add a little butter  
- If too thin, uncover pot to reduce
